# SpaceMonger for Mac privacy policy

SpaceMonger for Mac scans folders you choose on your Mac to show their disk usage. The scan results stay on your Mac. The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information, file names, file contents, or usage data with the developer or third parties.

The app can open an item in Finder or move an item to Trash when you choose those actions. macOS handles folder selection and may ask for permission to access protected locations.

For privacy questions, email [spacemonger@fothergill.com](mailto:spacemonger@fothergill.com).
